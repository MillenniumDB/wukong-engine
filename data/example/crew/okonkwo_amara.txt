MERIDIAN DEEP SPACE AGENCY
CREW DOSSIER

Name: Amara Okonkwo
Crew ID: MDSA-2071
Specialty: command
Status: active


BACKGROUND

Amara Okonkwo (MDSA-2071) joined the agency in 2178 from the orbital
construction service, where she had logged 340 hours of EVA on the Gateway
truss programme. She completed command certification in 2184 and holds
qualifications for Vesper-class and Aurora-class vehicles.

Her EVA record remains the longest of any serving crew member, and she is the
agency's designated authority on contingency hull repair.


FLIGHT RECORD

ARTEMIS-7 — mission commander. Okonkwo (MDSA-2071) held overall mission
authority for the deployment of the L2 Relay Array aboard MDV Cassiopeia
(VSP-03). She executed the L2 insertion burn and sequenced all four EVAs,
logging 22 hours 40 minutes of excursion time.

During ARTEMIS-7 she led the response to incident INC-0412, the aft equipment
bay hull breach, and completed the patch alongside Lt. Theo Vance (MDSA-2233)
on an unscheduled EVA. The review board credited her isolation call — made in
under 90 seconds — with preventing the loss of the bay's contents.

Prior to ARTEMIS-7 she served as flight engineer on two cislunar logistics
flights aboard the cargo tender MDV Halcyon (VSP-01).


CURRENT ASSIGNMENT

Okonkwo (MDSA-2071) is assigned to the agency's contingency operations office
and is not currently rostered to a flight. She contributed to the crew
rotation schedule adopted for KEPLER-12 and chairs the review of aft bay
shielding recommended in the ARTEMIS-7 outcome report.

She has indicated availability for a return to flight status from 2193.
