MERIDIAN DEEP SPACE AGENCY
CREW DOSSIER

Name: Mei Liang
Crew ID: MDSA-2190
Specialty: planetary science
Status: on flight


BACKGROUND

Dr. Mei Liang (MDSA-2190) joined the agency in 2181 from the Kuiper Survey
Consortium, where she led the ground-based programme that produced the target
catalogue the agency still flies against. She holds a doctorate in planetary
science and is dual-qualified as a flight medical officer.

She has served as science officer on every crewed deployment beyond cislunar
space flown since 2189.


FLIGHT RECORD

ARTEMIS-7 — science officer. Liang (MDSA-2190) completed the calibration
sequence for all three nodes of the L2 Relay Array and ran the habitability
instrument suite aboard MDV Cassiopeia (VSP-03). The habitability dataset she
returned was adopted by mission planning and informed the crew rotation
schedule later used on KEPLER-12.

She also maintained the medical log during ARTEMIS-7, including the crew
assessments following incident INC-0412.

KEPLER-12 — science officer. Liang (MDSA-2190) is responsible for the survey
instrument suite aboard MDV Perihelion (AUR-01). She has catalogued the
majority of the 1,184 bodies logged to date and has requested that the survey
boundary be extended from 58 AU to 61 AU on the strength of the instrument's
performance at low phase angles.

The 31-hour instrument gap caused by incident INC-0517 is recorded in her
observation log with a note dissenting from the power-down decision, on the
grounds that onboard storage would have preserved the record.


ASSESSMENT

Liang (MDSA-2190) is rated for continued flight. The board notes her
dissenting entry regarding INC-0517 and has referred the underlying question —
whether instruments may run without a downlink — to the operations standards
group.
