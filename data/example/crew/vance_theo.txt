MERIDIAN DEEP SPACE AGENCY
CREW DOSSIER

Name: Theo Vance
Crew ID: MDSA-2233
Specialty: piloting
Status: on flight


BACKGROUND

Theo Vance (MDSA-2233) joined the agency in 2183 as a test pilot. He holds
type ratings for every crewed vehicle currently in agency service and was the
first pilot certified on the Aurora-class survey vessel. He completed command
certification in 2190.

He is known within the corps for a preference for manual proximity operations,
a habit that originated during ARTEMIS-7 and has since been written into
agency training doctrine.


FLIGHT RECORD

ARTEMIS-7 — pilot. Vance (MDSA-2233) flew MDV Cassiopeia (VSP-03) through
trans-L2 injection and all three relay node approaches. After the automated
approach system produced an unacceptable closure rate on the first attempt, he
flew the remaining approaches manually. He assisted Cmdr. Amara Okonkwo
(MDSA-2071) with the hull patch following incident INC-0412.

KEPLER-12 — mission commander. Vance (MDSA-2233) was promoted to command for
the outer Kuiper belt survey aboard MDV Perihelion (AUR-01), and has held
mission authority since launch in January 2191.

During KEPLER-12 he took the decision to power down the survey instrument for
the duration of incident INC-0517, the 31-hour communications blackout, rather
than operate it without a downlink. The review board endorsed the decision.


ASSESSMENT

Vance (MDSA-2233) is rated for continued command. The board notes his
judgement under the loss of downlink during INC-0517 as exemplary and
recommends no change to his current assignment.
