MERIDIAN DEEP SPACE AGENCY
INCIDENT REPORT

Incident code: INC-0517
Title: High-gain antenna gimbal fault and communications blackout
Severity: minor
Date: 11 December 2192 (KEPLER-12 flight day 703)
Vehicle: MDV Perihelion (AUR-01)
Status: open


1. SUMMARY

On flight day 703 of KEPLER-12, the high-gain antenna of MDV Perihelion
(AUR-01) failed to track during a scheduled downlink window. The vehicle lost
its link to the deep-space network for 31 hours before the crew restored
pointing manually. No loss of vehicle control occurred at any point, and the
crew were never out of contact on the low-gain backup beacon.


2. CAUSE

The fault has been traced to a seized elevation bearing in the antenna
gimbal. The bearing comes from a supplier batch that was also fitted to the
crew transport MDV Cassiopeia (VSP-03), and the agency has opened a
fleet-wide review of the batch. A firmware contribution has not been ruled
out and is under investigation.


3. RESPONSE

Cmdr. Theo Vance (MDSA-2233) powered down the survey instrument for the
duration of the blackout rather than operate it without a downlink. Lt. Ravi
Sundaram (MDSA-2408) diagnosed the gimbal and restored pointing manually
after 31 hours. Telemetry buffered during the outage was recovered in full
once the link was re-established.

Dr. Mei Liang (MDSA-2190) entered a dissent in the observation log, arguing
that onboard storage would have preserved the particle-flux record and that
the instrument should have kept running.


4. CONSEQUENCES

The particle-flux record of KEPLER-12 carries a 31-hour gap. No catalogue
observations were lost, as the survey instrument was not scheduled to observe
during the affected window. The gimbal remains serviceable under manual
pointing with a reduced slew rate.


5. FINDINGS AND ACTIONS

The board endorses the power-down decision taken by Cmdr. Theo Vance
(MDSA-2233) as consistent with standing procedure.

The board refers the underlying question raised by Dr. Mei Liang (MDSA-2190) —
whether instruments may be operated without an active downlink — to the
operations standards group. The incident remains open pending the outcome of
the supplier batch review.
