MERIDIAN DEEP SPACE AGENCY
INCIDENT REPORT

Incident code: INC-0412
Title: Aft equipment bay hull breach
Severity: major
Date: 27 April 2189 (ARTEMIS-7 flight day 45)
Vehicle: MDV Cassiopeia (VSP-03)
Status: closed


1. SUMMARY

At 04:12 mission elapsed time on flight day 45 of ARTEMIS-7, a pressure alarm
annunciated in the aft equipment bay of MDV Cassiopeia (VSP-03). The bay lost
pressure at a rate consistent with a breach of approximately 4 mm. Cmdr. Amara
Okonkwo (MDSA-2071) isolated the bay within 90 seconds of the alarm. No crew
injury resulted and no pressurised crew volume was affected.


2. CAUSE

Post-flight inspection confirmed a micrometeoroid strike. The particle entered
through the outer shielding layer at an oblique angle and perforated the
pressure wall behind it. The impact site lies outside the region covered by
the uprated shielding fitted to the forward bays, a gap the design review had
accepted on the assumption of a shorter mission duration.


3. RESPONSE

The bay was isolated and vented. Node C release was deferred by six days while
the repair was planned. On flight day 47, Cmdr. Amara Okonkwo (MDSA-2071) and
Lt. Theo Vance (MDSA-2233) carried out an unscheduled EVA and applied a
two-layer patch over the impact site. Pressure integrity was confirmed over
the following 48 hours.

Dr. Mei Liang (MDSA-2190) recorded post-incident crew assessments in the
medical log. No physiological effects were noted.


4. CONSEQUENCES

Two experiment racks stowed in the aft bay were lost to vacuum exposure. The
L2 Relay Array deployment completed successfully despite the six-day slip, and
ARTEMIS-7 met all primary objectives.


5. FINDINGS AND ACTIONS

The board finds that the isolation call by Cmdr. Amara Okonkwo (MDSA-2071)
prevented the loss of the remaining bay contents and that the repair was
executed correctly.

The board recommends that aft bay shielding on all Vesper-class transports be
uprated to the forward-bay standard before any mission exceeding 120 days.
This recommendation is carried in the ARTEMIS-7 outcome report and the review
is chaired by Okonkwo (MDSA-2071).
