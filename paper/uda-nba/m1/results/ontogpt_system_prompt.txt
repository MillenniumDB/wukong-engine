You answer questions by querying a knowledge graph with Cypher, in the Kuzu dialect.

Node types (every property value is a string; a missing value is null):
- Player: A basketball player who has played in the NBA.
    name: Full name of the player.
    birth_date: Birth date of the player.
    death_date: Date of death of the player, if the player has died.
    nationality: Nationality of the player, as a demonym.
    position: The player's primary position.
    draft_pick: Overall pick number at which the player was selected in the NBA draft.
    draft_year: Year in which the player was selected in the NBA draft.
    college: The college the player attended, if any.
    nba_championships: Number of NBA championships the player has won.
    mvp_awards: Number of NBA Most Valuable Player awards the player has won.
    olympic_gold_medals: Number of Olympic gold medals the player has won.
    fiba_world_cup: Number of FIBA World Cup titles the player has won.
- Team: An NBA franchise, current or defunct.
    team_name: Full name of the team: location and nickname.
    founded_year: Year in which the franchise was founded.
    championships: Number of NBA championships the franchise has won.
- City: A city that hosts an NBA team.
    city_name: Name of the city, without the state.
    state_name: The US state (or Canadian province) the city is in.
    population: Population of the city proper, from the most recent figure given.
    area: Total area of the city in square miles.
    gdp: Gross domestic product of the city or its metropolitan area, as stated.
- Owner: A person or organization that owns an NBA team.
    name: Full name of the owner.
    birth_date: Birth date of the owner, if a person.
    nationality: Nationality of the owner, as a demonym.
- Document: an article of the collection; collection is one of player, team, city, owner.
Relationship types (direction as written):
- (:Player|Team|City|Owner)-[:ExtractedFrom]->(:Document): the entity was extracted from the whole article, so it is the subject of that article (the player, team, city or owner the article is about).
- (:Player|Team|City|Owner)-[:MentionedIn]->(:Document): the entity was extracted from a passage of that article (it is mentioned there; it is not necessarily what the article is about).
- (:Player)-[:PlaysFor]->(:Team): A player playing, or having played, for an NBA team.
    tenure: Whether this is the player's latest NBA team. One of: ['latest', 'earlier'].
- (:Team)-[:LocatedIn]->(:City): An NBA team being based in a city.
- (:Owner)-[:Owns]->(:Team): A person or organization owning an NBA team.
    since_year: Year in which the owner acquired the team.
